Our Approach

Ink & Iron isn't a walk-in tattoo shop with flash sheets on the wall. We're a small studio built around one idea: that a tattoo should mean something the day you get it and still mean something ten years later.

Every piece starts as a conversation. We ask questions — about the meaning, the placement, how the design will age with your skin. Then we draw it, send you a mockup, and revise it until it feels right. Only then do we book the session.

This is why we don't rush. Custom work takes time, and we'd rather turn down a project than half-do it.

What to Expect

1. Consultation (free, 20–30 min)

We meet, talk through your idea, look at reference images, and figure out placement, size, and budget. No commitment required.

2. Custom Design

Within a few days you receive a mockup. You can request changes — as many as needed — until the design feels right.

3. Deposit & Booking

A deposit locks in your appointment and comes off the final price. We book sessions in 2–6 hour blocks depending on the piece.

4. The Session

Private room, clean setup, sterilized equipment, and a comfortable chair. Snacks and water are on us. Breaks whenever you need them.

5. Aftercare

You leave with a written aftercare guide and a direct line to us if anything comes up during healing.

Common Questions

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, detail, placement, and time. Our hourly rate is $150, with a minimum charge of $100. During your consultation we'll give you a firm quote before any work begins.

Do I need a deposit?

Yes — a $50 deposit secures your appointment and comes off the final price. It's non-refundable but transferable if you reschedule at least 48 hours ahead.

How old do I need to be?

18 or older, no exceptions. We require valid government-issued photo ID on the day of your appointment.

Can I bring a friend?

One guest is welcome. We keep the studio calm, so large groups aren't a fit for long sessions.

Do you tattoo over scars or stretch marks?

Often yes, but it depends on the skin. Send a photo during your consultation and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

What if I need to cancel?

Life happens. Just give us at least 48 hours' notice and we'll move your deposit to a new date. No-shows forfeit the deposit.
